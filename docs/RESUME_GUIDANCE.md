# Resume guidance

An evidence-matched project bullet is:

> Engineered a fail-closed, reproducible five-metric evaluation harness for
> SDXL latent steering using FaceNet, LPIPS, MediaPipe, masked SSIM, and
> illumination-audited CIELAB measurements; on a legacy pilot sweep, measured
> a monotonic target response (ρ = -1.00) and a 0.908 mean quality rubric with
> 100% gate pass at moderate steering, while detecting identity and pose
> failures at extreme settings.

Keep “legacy pilot” in the bullet until the held-out 30-seed study and baselines
are complete. Do not use “proved bias mitigation,” “identity preserved,”
“disentangled,” or “state of the art.”
